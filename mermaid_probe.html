Mermaid 11 hexagon syntax probe

Verifies whether mermaid 11.12.0 accepts (/) inside an unquoted hexagon node label, and whether the quoted form id{{"label"}} renders the same label cleanly.

A. Unquoted hexagon, no parens (baseline — should render)

flowchart TD
    A{{wait_event:<br/>payment.completed<br/>timeout: 300s}}
flowchart TD A{{wait_event:<br/>payment.completed<br/>timeout: 300s}}

B. Unquoted hexagon WITH parens (current bug repro)

flowchart TD
    B{{wait_event:<br/>fworkflow.continue:(user_id)<br/>timeout: 600s}}
flowchart TD B{{wait_event:<br/>fworkflow.continue:(user_id)<br/>timeout: 600s}}

C. Quoted hexagon WITH parens (proposed fix)

flowchart TD
    C{{"wait_event:<br/>fworkflow.continue:(user_id)<br/>timeout: 600s"}}
flowchart TD C{{"wait_event:<br/>fworkflow.continue:(user_id)<br/>timeout: 600s"}}

D. Quoted hexagon keeping original braces (alt fix)

flowchart TD
    D{{"wait_event:<br/>f'workflow.continue:{user_id}'<br/>timeout: 600s"}}
flowchart TD D{{"wait_event:<br/>f'workflow.continue:{user_id}'<br/>timeout: 600s"}}
